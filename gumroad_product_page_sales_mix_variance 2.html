Frequently asked questions
What version of Excel do I need?
Excel 2016 or later (Windows or Mac). The file uses standard SUMIFS and IFERROR functions — no Power Query, no macros, no add-ins required. Also compatible with LibreOffice.
Can I add more SKUs or dimensions?
Yes. The formulas are built with open-ended SUMIFS ranges. Simply paste additional rows into the Input Area sheet and the calculations update automatically.
Is the methodology aligned with Big 4 / Fortune 500 standards?
Yes. The hierarchical weighted-average price decomposition is the standard approach used in FMCG commercial finance. Each mix level (Category → Brand → Pack → Channel) correctly uses the average price from the level above as the reference — ensuring components are additive and reconcile fully.
Can I use this for a real company's data?
Absolutely. Delete the sample data in the Input Area and paste your own Actual and Budget volumes, revenues, and variable costs. The model is built to handle real-world complexity including mixed SKU portfolios, partial periods, and new product launches.
